RQWST is a concept for coordinating media requests among journalists, publicists, sources, stakeholders, and the tools that support them.

From here on, this content-reduced demo uses illustrative data to show RQWST's identity on design-system-ASK. It is not a product specification or an official RQWST publication.

Sequence

An illustrative sequence for a request like the sample above.

arrive >> route >> book >> answer >> file

  1. step 1

    Arrive

    A question comes in with its topic, outlet and deadline.

  2. step 2

    Route

    It goes to the person best placed to answer it.

  3. step 3

    Book

    A time is agreed for the comment or the interview.

  4. step 4

    Answer

    The comment is delivered before the deadline.

  5. step 5

    File

    The exchange is kept for reference.

About this demo

This page is a worked example of design-system-ASK. It opens with RQWST's public landing page, as rqwst.com showed it on 14 November 2026, set in the design system's roles; its sign-in, application, terms and contact links lead to rqwst.com. The label above its headline, and the concept example below it, were written for this demo. The design system supplies the interface unchanged: type, color, the page shell and its navigation, the compact actions, the panel material and the document register with its tables, in light and dark. RQWST supplies its identity: the wordmark, the plume that opens the navigation, and the lavender field of the light theme.

  • Open the navigation from the plume: top left on a desktop, bottom right on a phone.
  • Switch the system between light and dark; the page follows it.
  • Print the page; it keeps its roles and composition on paper.

The landing section's copy, apart from its label, is RQWST's own, from rqwst.com. The rest of the page copy was written for this public demo, and every sample on it is illustrative and fictional.
